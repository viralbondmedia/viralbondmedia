Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started as a brand?

Simply fill out our brand registration form. We'll review your requirements and match you with the most relevant influencers within 48 hours.

What types of influencers do you work with?

We work with micro and macro influencers across niches — tech, beauty, gaming, lifestyle, fitness, food, and more. All creators are verified for authentic engagement.

How are payments handled?

Payments are secure and transparent. Brands pay only for confirmed deliverables, and creators receive payments on time through our escrow-protected system.

Can I choose specific influencers?

Absolutely! While our algorithm suggests the best matches, you have full control to browse, review, and select the creators that align with your brand vision.

What platforms do you support?

We support campaigns across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and emerging platforms. Multi-platform strategies are our specialty.
